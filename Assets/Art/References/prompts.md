# 横版三地图块玩法示意

内置 image_gen 生成。构图：左侧完整地图块—中间缺失地图块—右侧完整地图块，三块横向排列；每块设计规格 16×16 格，临时角色 1×1 格。侧面观察的横版 2D 洞穴平台场景，无俯视道路。图片为美术及玩法概念，正式 Tilemap 应校准网格。

## 暖色矿洞

文件：01-warm-mineshaft.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE wide landscape pixel-art gameplay concept image, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. This is a SIDE-VIEW 2D PLATFORMER cave exploration puzzle, orthographic front elevation, horizontal gameplay with gravity pointing DOWN. Never overhead, never isometric, never a bird's-eye map.

NON-NEGOTIABLE COMPOSITION: exactly THREE identical SQUARE map chunk positions placed side-by-side in ONE horizontal row, with no other chunk, thumbnail, inset, HUD, or second row. LEFT position is a complete square cave chunk. MIDDLE position is one entire MISSING SQUARE CHUNK represented by a dark empty square with a fine dashed amber border and a very subtle 16x16 grid. RIGHT position is a complete square cave chunk. All three squares have exactly equal width and height, share the same top and bottom boundaries, and fill the wide canvas. Make the absent middle chunk visually obvious, do not fill it with scenery, a bridge, or a completed terrain chunk.

Each square chunk represents 16 by 16 TILE UNITS, not 16x16 image pixels. One tiny provisional player on LEFT occupies exactly ONE tile wide and ONE tile tall, approximately 1/16 of chunk width and height. Simple hooded side-facing silhouette, no finalized character design. Player stands upright on a horizontal walkable rock floor looking RIGHT toward the missing chunk. One collectible small gold parchment map shard nearby in the left room. Each complete chunk has a dark rocky ceiling near its top, stalactites, open cave air in the middle, a horizontal solid rock platform floor at about 11/16 of the chunk height, and layered rock beneath. The left floor ENDS at the left edge of the missing square; the right floor BEGINS at the right edge of the missing square at EXACTLY THE SAME HEIGHT, so filling the blank middle with a matching chunk would connect a walkable horizontal route. NO diagonal overhead paths, no winding top-down roads. Exit is a small bright daylight cave mouth at the far right end of the right chunk's floor, with enough headroom for the 1x1 character. Decorative features stay subordinate to readable platforms. Restrained amber highlights at the two exposed floor endpoints imply compatible map connections. The EMPTY MIDDLE square has no floor: a gap the player cannot cross until placing the missing full square chunk. No movement arrows or floating block previews. This one image illustrates the pre-placement puzzle state.

Style: handcrafted pixel art, crisp square pixel clusters and hard edges, consistent resolution and limited palette, no antialiasing, no smooth gradients, no photorealism or 3D. Pure gameplay mockup, no words, numbers, captions, labels, title, logos, watermark, or UI. The only square panels are the three required chunk positions.

Art direction: Warm retro mining cave. Umber and chocolate rock, ochre horizontal earth floor, subtle wooden support beams and one amber lantern. Small teal mineral accents. Friendly mysterious exploration mood. Dark navy tiny player silhouette with one pale face pixel. Golden daylight at right exit. Visually restrained, readable, classic 16-bit pixel game.
